## Opening stock delivery recovery and visibility

The live Product creation failure `EXECUTION_UNCONFIRMED` was traced to eight retained Accounting stock terminal outbox events that had exhausted the old 12-attempt limit. Their first delivery failed because Identity rejected an unsupported mixed stock and journal feature set. After the feature contract fix, three abort events also conflicted with an older reservation payload; terminal abort now first uses the retained operation identity without reconstructing that payload, and supplies the validated intent only if Identity has no reservation. Only the eight verified events were reset for retry. All eight subsequently published; the affected company now reports `pending=False` from `stock_opening_control.pending` and the retained reservations are terminal. No ledger data was manually changed.

The Accounting stock terminal publisher now retries without a fixed attempt cutoff, with delays capped at five minutes, and logs an error every minute for a backlog older than one minute. Catalog translates the internal `EXECUTION_UNCONFIRMED` code into an actionable Product opening message. This keeps a message available to resume after a code or dependency fix; it does not repair the cause automatically or page an operator by itself. The 14 unpublished `identity.posting_reservation.terminal` records found in a separate live inventory target the documented future Billing integration, whose consumer is not deployed; they are not evidence of another current Product opening failure. A platform-wide count of silent delivery paths has not been established by this scoped review.
