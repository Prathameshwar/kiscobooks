# Sales voucher configuration implementation plan

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Status:** Sales policy and shared non-statutory numbering configuration slices implemented; legal Sales numbering and the remaining delivery slices and UI are pending. **Scope:** Sales and Sales-derived types/classes, with India GST as the first statutory profile. Payroll is excluded. Read with [commercial configuration](voucher-commercial-configuration.md), [all-kind configuration](voucher-configuration-design.md), [invoice calculation](voucher-invoice-calculation.md), [tax recording](voucher-tax-recording.md), and the [delivery tracker](voucher-delivery-tracker.md).

## 1. TallyPrime findings and platform interpretation

This is based on current official TallyHelp documentation, not a running TallyPrime installation. Tally's company features, voucher-type settings, voucher classes, F12 entry options and print settings are different layers. Its entry/display switches must not become authority to omit required posting or tax facts here.

## 2. Current platform baseline and gaps

**Already present:** `voucher_type`, `voucher_type_revision`, `voucher_class`, shared `voucher_configuration_profile/revision/binding`, Sales-family binding, an effective-configuration endpoint and digest, common voucher/line/item/invoice/component/quantity/tender/bill-allocation tables, and a delegated Accounting invoice preview. `InvoicePolicy` permits supply kinds, price bases, component scopes/inputs, sales account nature, broad manual discount/charge/free/deal booleans, plus bounded Sales party/supply account allowlists, tax profiles, discount/charge scopes, charge purposes, tender modes and settlement gates. The invoice input stores goods/service/account supplies, component targets/dependencies, actual/billed quantities, tax context, tenders, and advance allocations. These are foundations, not proof that all combinations post correctly.

**Current limits:** policy does not yet express party-group eligibility, price sources and overrides, per-purpose component caps or defaults, quantity differences, independent gifts, default charge/tax/rounding recipes, source-linked fulfillment or statutory evidence. Account allowlists and settlement gates cover only a subset of the intended rules. Type/class defaults and configuration inheritance need explicit default-versus-restriction semantics; contradictory policies must fail at publication. Current invoice acceptance is `STANDARD` only. Captured tax profiles are bounded to untaxed account and India domestic service/RCM service contexts; goods, export and broader statutory execution must remain gated. B15-B18 are in progress; B24 goods posting, B25 returns and B19 statutory artifacts are not verified. No activation checkpoint is complete.

## 3. Configuration contract

Use the existing Vouchers company `SALES` configuration revision as the envelope, then Sales type revision and class. The executable kind/profile, Identity capabilities and actor permission, Catalog master versions, Accounting tax/stock capability and company-year state bound the result. A class may default a permitted value, but cannot legalize an otherwise forbidden behavior. Treat `availability`, `requiredness`, `default`, `editability`, `override permission/reason`, and `approval threshold` as separate properties. Publish immutable effective revisions and pin their IDs/hash to each document and accepted calculation. Never reinterpret a posted invoice using today's settings.

| Family | Typed controls and rules to implement |
|---|---|
| Type/entry | Supported `ACCOUNTS`/`ITEMS`/`MIXED` modes, default mode, versioned numbering-series policy (detailed below), cash/credit/POS/export subtype, required sections and custom header/item fields. Do not expose a mode without a verified posting recipe. |
| Party/accounts | Allowed customer groups and account natures; customer, bill-to, consignee/ship-to roles; one common income ledger or per-supply ledger rules, with item/group defaults. Validate active, company-scoped account and party master versions; no arbitrary GL account or silent fallback. |
| Pricing | Allowed Catalog price levels/books and effective dates, quantity tier basis, currency/FX source, net or tax-inclusive entry, manual-rate override ceiling and permission/reason. Snapshot selected price/master revisions. |
| Quantities/stock | Actual/billed separation, allowed difference reasons, zero-value gift/sample permission, warehouse/lot/serial/expiry requirements, negative-stock rejection, direct versus linked dispatch, and same-item/cross-item reward scope. A warning preference never weakens stock admission. |
| Discounts/charges | Distinct line, selected-line and invoice discount permissions; fixed/percent/per-quantity inputs; ordered dependency bases; eligible lines and stacking; allowed charge definitions/accounts, targets, amount caps and default/mandatory status; rounding bounds and ledger. Preserve commercial, tax-base and stock-cost allocations separately. |
| Tax | Supported registration and domestic/export supply profile, item/ledger tax classification, place of supply, inclusive/exclusive basis, multi-component GST/cess, charge assessability, tax timing and document requirements. Tax override needs a supported legal case and evidence, not a generic `allow_tax_edit`. State VAT is a later jurisdiction adapter, not a second manual tax mode. |
| Settlement | Credit due-date and bill-reference rules, immediate cash/bank/POS tenders, split modes, payment reference requirements, advance application, limits and overpayment handling. Bill allocations and receipt origin remain authoritative; gateway support is separately gated. |
| Source/output | Sales order/quotation/challan links and partial-fulfillment caps; dispatch/export/transport evidence; e-invoice/e-way bill eligibility/artifact status; terms, declarations and print template. Source facts and statutory fields are validated on the API even when the future form hides them. |

Shared storage remains mandatory: extend versioned policy JSON with a **typed, schema-versioned Sales contract** and reference tables only where indexed identity or one-to-many defaults require them; reuse existing invoice, item, component, tax, tender and source-allocation structures. No `sales_voucher` or per-type tables. A company-defined Sales type inherits these capabilities only through a registered executable profile. Custom attributes use the existing typed header/item-field mechanism and cannot drive tax/posting without a native field mapping.

The effective-policy response should expose source revision IDs, allowed/required/default/editable controls and reasons for disabled mechanics. Add draft validation and impact preview to the existing configuration/type/class APIs, with expected-version publish and dated binding. At document create/edit, invoice preview/accept, approval and issue, re-evaluate the pinned policy and current hard gates. Reject unsupported or stale inputs atomically; idempotent replay returns the original result. Separate document calculation/approval from external statutory artifact success.

### 3.1 Sales numbering and financial-year contract

TallyPrime offers Automatic, Automatic (Manual Override), Manual and Multi-user Auto numbering; automatic series can specify a starting number, numeric width/zero fill, effective-dated prefix/suffix, and periodic restarts, including a new financial year. Its override example advances the next suggested number after a higher manual choice. [Numbering methods](https://help.tallysolutions.com/use-voucher-numbering-methods/), [new-year setup](https://help.tallysolutions.com/moving-to-the-next-financial-year-india-change-current-period/). We adopt those useful controls without adopting Tally's option to renumber old issued documents or reuse a released number: the [financial-year contract](voucher-financial-year-design.md#5-numbering-identity-and-fiscal-year-resets) requires immutable assignments and cancellation history.

**Existing support and gap:** The shared non-statutory `voucher_number_series` now stores prefix, suffix, padding, start, numbering mode, reset policy and optional year in the display. Its issue command accepts a manual full number or numeric override as appropriate; the server checks format, role and override reason, retains issued assignments, and maintains a high-water counter for automatic numbering. The effective voucher policy already has allowed/default series IDs. This rule is still creation-only rather than versioned/date-effective, lacks legal issuer/category scope and cross-series collision control, and uses a single `fiscal_start_date` field as the reset key (a fixed sentinel for continuous numbering). Sales invoice issuance is explicitly rejected in Vouchers pending the statutory profile; this shared counter must not be mistaken for a ready legal Sales invoice allocator. Accounting's internal `document_sequence` is also a separate identity.

**Required configuration:** A versioned shared series rule selected by company Sales policy and narrowed by Sales type/class shall contain: numbering mode `AUTOMATIC | AUTOMATIC_WITH_OVERRIDE | MANUAL` (multi-user automatic uses the same atomic server allocator), allowed/default series, applicable-from/to dates, issuer/registration and legal document category, number-date basis, starting positive integer, width/zero-fill, literal prefix and suffix, and reset schedule (`FINANCIAL_YEAR`, `NEVER`, or a separately supported dated period). The baseline numeric increment is exactly **1**; an arbitrary step would create deliberate gaps and is outside this release. For `NEVER`, the counter namespace persists across company years; for `FINANCIAL_YEAR`, a new namespace starts at the configured starting number. Do not infer the legal GST year from the text in a prefix or from the GL posting year. A class may select a permitted series/default but cannot change its legal scope, formatting or uniqueness rule.

**Issuance semantics:** `AUTOMATIC` allocates the next number under an owner lock. `AUTOMATIC_WITH_OVERRIDE` accepts an explicit positive numeric sequence only with permission and reason; the server formats it using the effective prefix/suffix and advances the next candidate to `max(previous_next, override + 1)` within that reset period, while lower unused numbers are never automatically reused. `MANUAL` requires a complete number on every issue, validated against the published series format, permitted character/length and legal uniqueness scope; a manual value does not silently alter an automatic counter. Duplicate prevention is mandatory for issued Sales documents, even where Tally exposes a toggle. Reject out-of-range, duplicate or stale-rule numbers before issue; quote previews are provisional and do not reserve numbers. An idempotent retry returns the original assignment. Cancellation voids the identity and does not free it. Never rewrite a posted/issued number when a series is edited or a late document is inserted.

The statutory Sales number allocator belongs to Accounting under D05, in the same atomic accepted-posting/issuance contract as its legal evidence; Vouchers may hold the configured rule and the non-statutory order/quote/challan series but must not independently allocate a second Sales invoice number. Assignment stores the full immutable number, numeric sequence if applicable, rule revision, issuer/registration, legal category, date basis, statutory year/reset key and issue status. Uniqueness uses the legal issuer/registration + document category + statutory year + **full number**, not merely type ID, suffix or numeric part. Validate India GST's full-number format/length under the applicable adapter and keep supplier/customer references separate. A March posting performed in April uses the legal document-date year and its own eligible series; a new April invoice uses the next statutory year if the enabled profile says so. Both may be issued concurrently under distinct locked namespaces. For example, a configured `SI/25-26/` + `0001` and `SI/26-27/` + `0001` can coexist as different full numbers and year keys; a March invoice cannot acquire the April series merely because it was entered in April.

**APIs and checks:** Extend the shared series draft/validate/publish/history and effective-configuration responses, issue command (`requested_sequence` or `manual_number` only for its matching mode), and owner assignment result. Keep all type/series changes versioned and date-effective. On publish, detect overlapping rules and cross-type collisions for the same legal scope; on issue, recheck period readiness, registration/date binding, active rule, permission, complete-number validity and uniqueness atomically. Include two-year rollover, backdated/late issue, concurrent writers, override/high-water, manual duplicate, cancellation, retry and rule-change cases in B08-compatible regression tests and B16-B19/B45 acceptance; do not mark statutory Sales numbering verified from the current B08 order/quote/challan implementation.

## 4. Delivery slices and acceptance

**Implementation checkpoint (2026-10-11):** The shared `InvoicePolicy` now accepts bounded Sales party/supply account allowlists, tax profiles, separate discount/charge scopes, charge purposes, tender modes, and credit/split-tender/advance/invoice-rounding gates. Sales-only controls are rejected on Purchase/common configuration; active account references are checked against company-scoped Vouchers projections. Capture and Accounting owner preview enforce the same Sales-only rules. Non-credit Sales requires exact payable settlement at the owner after tax/discount calculation. Default omission preserves prior policy/calculation hashes. This is only a subset of slice 1 and the account/service enforcement in slice 3. Price-source selection, party/group policies, component caps/defaults, goods/POS/returns, statutory numbering and artifacts remain unimplemented; no Sales activation is implied.

**Numbering checkpoint (2026-10-11):** Creation of shared non-statutory series now accepts suffix, start value, `AUTOMATIC`, `AUTOMATIC_WITH_OVERRIDE` or `MANUAL`, financial-year or continuous reset, and optional fiscal-start year in the display. Issue validates manual formatting, override permission/reason, duplicate sequence and automatic high-water progression. The old default display remains unchanged. These rules support quotation/order/challan-style issue identities; they do not allocate a legal Sales invoice number. Effective-dated immutable rule revisions, legal registration/category uniqueness, GST format/length and Accounting-owned statutory assignment remain open under D05/D12.

**QuickBooks-informed workflow checkpoint (2026-10-11):** Sparse schema-v2
`sales_workflow` now requires a nonblank external customer-order reference when
configured and restricts the Sales invoice due date to a bounded window from
document date. This improves captured terms and issue evidence, but does not
verify or consume an order, support progress invoicing or alter tax/GL posting.
See the [focused coverage review](voucher-quickbooks-coverage-plan.md).
