# India FMCG backend implementation plan

**Prepared:** 2026-10-12. **Status:** plan with two partial FM1 source slices; no FMCG goods profile is activated by this document. The [voucher delivery tracker](voucher-delivery-tracker.md) remains the authoritative batch/status record; the [FMCG checklist](fmcg-business-implementation-todo.md) tracks this work. Read with the [stock design](voucher-stock-recording-reporting-design.md), [invoice calculation](voucher-invoice-calculation.md), [commercial configuration](voucher-commercial-configuration.md), [order execution contract](voucher-phase5-execution.md), [Catalog pricing design](catalog-pricing-design.md), [company/item configuration plan](company-item-configuration-plan.md), and [pharmacy plan](pharmacy-chemist-implementation-plan.md). This plan adds FMCG acceptance scope to those owners; it does not create another service, posting authority, voucher aggregate, or stock balance.

## 1. Outcome, profiles, and current boundary

The first operational target is an India-based retailer, wholesaler, or distributor of packaged food, beverages, personal care, household and other ordinary FMCG goods. It must buy by case or other purchase pack; receive identifiable product/declaration cohorts; hold and move stock across stores/depots; sell by permitted units and channels; issue a GST-supported document; collect cash/bank/credit; handle a partial return, damaged/expired stock and supplier claim; and reconcile item quantity/value, tax and party balances. Food and other date-sensitive categories additionally need licence, shelf-life, hold and recall controls. Manufacturer/contract-packer and principal-owned C&F profiles are separately activated extensions, not silently inferred from a trader's stock.

The present code has item units/barcodes, price levels, dated MRP defaults, warehouse/inventory definitions, manual invoice component controls, dormant same-item promotion facts and non-posting order capture. The [Catalog effective read](../apis/catalog/src/catalog/api/product_authoring.py) still reports goods stock execution `UNAVAILABLE`; [Vouchers capabilities](../apis/vouchers/src/vouchers/api/routes.py) report `stock_execution=false`; [Accounting invoice execution](../apis/accounting/src/accounting/tasks/invoice_execution.py) refuses goods lines. B15-B18 remain in progress; B19-B27 and B29-B32 are not verified; B28 has capture only. Do not advertise purchase receipt, dispatch, goods invoice, stock availability, automatic scheme or stock report as executable on the strength of a definition or draft.

The existing customer > customer-type > company price-level resolver is reusable. The channel-pricing defect identified at planning time has a local FM1 repair slice: channel authoring/resolution, a normalized unique key and generated API contract. Catalog also has a reviewed, effective-dated FOOD category source and signed Vouchers projection. Focused and native MySQL checks pass for these slices. Downstream issue-time consumers, FSSAI premises/operation evidence and the rest of FM1 remain open; these source slices do not activate FMCG prices or food issuance.

## 2. Common data and effect contract

| Owner | Native records and responsibility |
|---|---|
| Identity | Company, fiscal year, branch/premises, GST registration, dated statutory licence/registration evidence, feature activation and issue-time company readiness. Food licence applicability is scoped to the actual business/premises and operation. |
| Catalog | SKU/brand/variant, group/category, base and alternate unit revision, purchase/sale pack, barcode/GTIN, HSN/tax classification, handling and expiry policy, warehouse/replenishment policy, price plan/level/channel/party scope, product MRP default and published promotion definitions. Catalog never owns on-hand or receipt-specific printed MRP. |
| Accounting | Party/ship-to/commercial category, credit and bill balances; one guarded stock authority for positions, lots, receipt/declaration cohorts, movements, cost layers/average pools, holds, reservations if selected, value/GL/tax effects and source-capped claims. The physical cohort stores actual printed MRP, pack/net quantity, manufacturer/packer/importer when relevant, batch, dates, source and evidence. A lot may contain multiple MRP cohorts. |
| Vouchers | Shared voucher header/revision/line and optional typed sections for invoice, fulfillment, physical allocation, transport, tender and source claim. Planned, actual, billed, free/reward, returned and damaged quantities remain distinct. A document states whether it `EXECUTES`, `REFERENCES` or `DEFERS` a physical effect. |
| Reports | Frozen, permission-checked owner feeds for stock/price/expiry/claims, account/party/tax and order/fulfillment registers. Reports never supplies posting-time availability or a second editable balance. |

The canonical stock position and immutable movement/value contract are those in the stock design. Case/inner/each/weight conversions pin the exact item-unit revision used; changing a pack ratio never reinterprets old stock. Sale price, supplier discount, MRP and inventory cost are different facts. The same invoice may allocate a bill-level discount, a freight/handling charge and multiple tax components to different items/cohorts, but one owner calculation and one transaction commit determine payable, tax, stock cost and GL. Custom attributes may hold descriptive FMCG information; quantities, dates, legal evidence, valuation, scheme entitlements and balances use native typed fields.

## 3. Phase order and detailed delivery

Each phase is backend-first and ends with migrations, models, schemas, APIs, signed projections/events, permissions, audit, OpenAPI/generated clients, focused and native database tests, recovery evidence and updated batch/todo status where applicable. Do not advance a parent B batch because an FMCG sub-slice passes. Independent definition work may proceed early; execution follows the existing B dependencies.

### FM0 — Reconcile scope and close dependent contracts

**Existing gates:** D01-D07, D09, D11-D12; B15-B19; B20 stock manifest; B31 selection. Review current source and tracker evidence before any migration. Freeze a profile matrix for (a) ordinary retail, (b) wholesale/distributor/owned-stockist, (c) food/date-sensitive goods, (d) manufacturer/contract packer, and (e) principal-owned custody. For each, record who owns goods, whether a voucher moves stock, tax document/registration, price/credit/tender mode, return treatment, premises/licence rule and unsupported cases. Record the supported Indian GST and packaged-commodity rule versions from authoritative sources; do not hardcode an e-way/e-invoice turnover threshold or assume every FMCG product needs batch/expiry.

Decide B31 stock and credit reservations separately. **Recommendation:** select stock reservation for wholesale confirmed orders, because promising and allocating stock otherwise cannot be atomic; keep simple counter retail non-reserving. This is a proposed D03/D06 policy, not a change to the tracker until reviewed. Close FIFO and moving-average D02 cost examples, receipt-cohort MRP identity, zero-cost versus unknown-cost stock, and source-claim/lock order before B20/B23/B24 migrations. Exit: an owner/schema/API/permission/event manifest, example calculations and incompatible-case refusal matrix are reviewable; no activation flag changes.

**FM0 profile/effect matrix (draft; decisions and statutory applicability still open):**

| Profile | Goods owner and physical effect | Sale/settlement and tax evidence | Return and restriction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary non-food retail | Company-owned stock; receipt, transfer and direct sale move actual units only after B20-B24. Quote/order never moves stock. | STORE or other authorized channel; tender and any credit are captured separately. Issuer registration, place of supply, rate and document profile must be resolved for the supply. | Original-source-capped paid/free return; no food-only licence or expiry requirement. |
| Wholesale/distributor/owned stockist | Company-owned depot stock; order records demand, challan records physical dispatch, invoice either references that movement or executes a direct sale once. Reservation is an explicit B31 choice. | Customer, ship-to, channel/price level and credit terms are dated source facts. GST issuer/recipient, supply/transport and calculation evidence are pinned before issue. | Partial dispatch/bill/return remain separate; wrong outlet, hold or over-claim blocks execution. |
| Food/date-sensitive trader | Same owner/effect rules as retail or wholesale, with lot/declaration cohort and FEFO/minimum-life where configured. | FOOD category and exact issuing/handling premises plus licensed operation are required when applicable; FSSAI evidence and GST facts are separate. | Recall/hold blocks only affected stock; missing or stale evidence blocks food execution, while non-food remains eligible. |
| Manufacturer/contract packer | Own inputs/WIP/outputs or explicitly principal-owned custody; transformation is B37-B41/C4, not an invoice side effect. | BOM/yield/cost and the issuer's tax treatment depend on ownership and job-work direction. | Scrap/by-product and returns preserve genealogy; principal stock is never silently capitalized as company stock. |
| Principal-owned C&F custodian | Principal retains ownership; custody movement and commission/settlement are distinct from the principal's goods sale. | Agreement identifies issuer, premises, tax document, commission and source claims. | Custody shortages/returns are source-capped; no inferred transfer of title or automatic principal invoice. |

This matrix is an acceptance contract, not an activation selection. Exact GST/food/legal rule versions, FIFO/average cost scope, B31 reservation and owner lock order remain subject to their existing decision/evidence gates.

### FM1 — Product, party, premises and channel readiness

**Owners:** Catalog, Identity, Accounting parties, Vouchers source projections. **Maps to:** CI-P1/P2/P3, B15-B19 source prerequisites. Complete SKU/variant/brand, pack/inner/case/each or measured units, pack ratios/decimal grids, alternate barcodes, HSN and sell/purchase restrictions with dated versions. A pack or assortment with its own barcode, MRP or tax/quantity behavior must have an explicit sellable identity; an arbitrary JSON attribute cannot silently act as one. Preserve item group history and warehouse permissions. Record food/date-sensitive classification and handling profile separately from drug classification; resolve FSSAI registration/licence by company premises, operation and effective date when applicable. Do not block non-food goods on food-only fields.

Repair channel pricing end to end: add typed channel scope to series commands/reads and resolution, review the unique/FK/index design and fallback precedence, and test simultaneous STORE/WHOLESALE/MARKETPLACE prices for one SKU, unit and date. Retain party > party-type > company price level, then apply the reviewed channel/pack scope and explicit fallback. Define PTS/PTR/retail/reference and negotiated supplier/customer terms as named rate sources, not a fixed-margin derivation; preserve floor/ceiling and approval evidence. Exit: a protected, issue-time-ready SKU/party/premises/price snapshot with exact source revisions; stale/missing classification, wrong pack or unauthorized channel fails closed.

### FM2 — Stock core, eligibility and valuation

**Owner:** Accounting, consuming Catalog/Identity/Vouchers snapshots. **Maps to:** B20-B22, D01-D03/D07/D12. Implement canonical position uniqueness, stock posting/immutable signed movements, lot and independent receipt-origin records, guarded balances, hold history, stock-period controls, source receipts and recovery. Add lot/receipt cohorts that can share manufacturer batch but differ by supplier receipt, purchase cost, printed MRP or pack declaration. Support controlled opening stock with known/assumed/unknown provenance. Receipt and issue enforce company, owner/custody, warehouse/bin, exact base quantity, negative-stock `BLOCK`, date/expiry/minimum-life and hold checks under one lock order.

Implement both selected valuation methods, FIFO and moving average, with separate physical receipt-age allocation, immutable cost/value legs, landed cost, zero-cost evidence, GL/clearing links and correction constraints. FEFO selects eligible physical lots; it is not a valuation method. Exit: concurrent receipts/holds/issues cannot create duplicate positions or sell held stock; quantities and value replay from immutable records; FIFO/average and year-crossing examples reconcile to GL. Keep `SPECIFIC`, `WARN` and `ALLOW` unexecutable until separately proven.

### FM3 — Procurement, receiving and physical movement

**Owners:** Vouchers and Accounting. **Maps to:** B23, B28-B30 procurement portions, B16/B19 statutory dependencies. Implement purchase order -> partial receipt note -> purchase invoice, direct purchase invoice/receipt, supplier short/excess/rejection and depot/branch transit. Pin supplier, ship-from, receiving premises, document, unit revision, actual quantity, batch/dates and receipt-cohort pack/MRP evidence. Validate source identity, pack declaration and supplier term before creating stock. Split physical receipt from financial bill: a later purchase invoice `REFERENCES` the committed receipt and allocates freight, discounts, eligible tax and landed cost without another stock movement. An invoice-first path uses only a supported deferred/clearing profile.

Exit: 100 cases ordered, 60 received in two cohorts, 55 invoiced, 5 rejected and 40 outstanding produce exact independent quantities and value; duplicate retry or second supplier invoice cannot re-receive; across-year claims persist. Supplier free units increase actual stock and preserve cost/tax/claim purpose instead of disappearing into a discount total.

### FM4 — Retail and wholesale sales execution

**Owners:** Vouchers and Accounting. **Maps to:** B15-B19, B24, B28-B30. Implement direct counter sale and order -> pick -> partial delivery challan -> invoice. Record the actual cohort picked; validate its printed MRP and any allowed pack conversion against the legally comparable tax-inclusive selling amount for applicable packaged goods. A current Catalog MRP is a candidate/default, never a replacement for the physical pack's declaration. Price-level/channel/party agreement, manual line/bill discounts, charges, GST and rounding are resolved through the single accepted calculation hash and owner recomputation. Goods sales move actual units and all-unit COGS once; invoice and challan references cannot move them again.

Retail backend supports barcode lookup, mixed cash/bank/digital/credit tender, receipt identity, refunds and a controlled cash-session reconciliation; a future POS UI can use the same API. Wholesale backend supports ship-to, agreed rate/credit terms, partial dispatch, transport evidence, invoice issue and bill allocation. Apply dated GST profiles/registrations and statutory document requirements from B16/B19; distinguish GST supply/value date from stock and GL dates. Exit: split pack/lot invoices, mixed tender, one challan invoiced twice, stale price/registration, wrong warehouse and above-cohort-MRP cases have deterministic results/refusals; stock, COGS, party, tax and tender totals bridge exactly.

### FM5 — Distribution demand, allocation and replenishment

**Owners:** Vouchers, Accounting, Reports. **Maps to:** B28-B32, B31 if selected. Complete requisition/quotation/order acceptance, delivery schedules, order-source conversions, exact per-line claims, partial/consolidated dispatch, backorder, cancellation, short-close/reopen and order-to-movement-to-bill links. Quotations and orders remain non-posting. For selected wholesale reservation, Accounting atomically reserve/release/consume eligible stock and enforce expiry, holds and competing claims; retail availability remains on-hand minus committed reservations where applicable. Model outlet/ship-to and optional territory/route/beat/sales-representative references as typed, dated distribution dimensions without introducing payroll.

Replenishment uses authoritative on-hand, reserved, committed inbound, demand, lead time, order multiples and safety stock at an explicit horizon. Unknown/missing producer feeds return `UNKNOWN`, never a fabricated zero. Exit: two simultaneous customers cannot receive the same promised case; partial schedules and year-crossing orders reconcile ordered/executed/billed/returned/closed; reorder proposals explain every input and do not count transit or future receipts as available now.

### FM6 — Promotions, returns, claims and tax corrections

**Owners:** Catalog publication, Vouchers capture, Accounting execution, Reports. **Maps to:** B15-B18, B24-B27, B30. Publish bounded, versioned sales schemes with complete owner acknowledgement and atomic entitlement/redemption. Start with same-item 10+1; then add cross-item, bundle/quantity/slab and supplier-funded mechanics only with reviewed allocation/tax/stock contracts. Keep manual bonus, paid bundle, independent gift/sample, cash discount and supplier rebate as distinct purposes. The free/reward quantity moves stock and carries cost even when its commercial line value is zero. CBIC [Circular 92/11/2019-GST](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/circular-cgst-92.pdf) distinguishes paid bundles from independent gifts/samples and describes discount conditions; tax treatment must follow the selected purpose and source evidence, not a generic `free=true` flag.

Source-link customer returns, supplier returns, expiry/breakage/shortage claims and replacements to original paid/reward, lot/cohort, tax, discount, charges and stock-cost allocations. Cap cumulative claims under lock; receive damaged/expired goods into quarantine, not saleable stock. Supplier commercial settlement and tax credit/debit note are separate effects; original invoice benefit is reversed proportionally rather than recomputed from today's scheme. Exit: 10+1 issue moves 11 and costs 11, partial return of paid/reward units cannot overclaim, a supplier expiry claim does not add stock or GST twice, and concurrent claims conserve original quantity/value/tax.

### FM7 — Food quality, recall and other controlled FMCG categories

**Owners:** Identity/Catalog source facts, Accounting holds/trace, Vouchers evidence, Reports. **Depends on:** FM2-FM6 physical lineage. For food profiles, record applicable premises FSSAI evidence, product/batch/pack identity, manufacture/packing/best-before/expiry facts, storage requirements, supplier and downstream recipient trace. Implement lot/cohort/location/partial-quantity holds, recall/disposal/release approvals, evidence hashes and protected correction history. FEFO and minimum remaining life are item/profile controlled; non-perishable categories do not inherit a forced expiry field. A cold-chain variant requires measured excursion-to-hold execution and calibration/source evidence before activation, not merely a temperature setting.

The FSSAI [Food Recall Procedure Regulations](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Food_Recall_Regulations.pdf) call for distribution records including supplier/customer, lot/batch, pack and date details and stopping distribution during recall; the [FSSAI licensing page](https://fssai.gov.in/business/licensing) describes food-business registration/licensing. Exit: recalling one batch identifies every affected receipt, depot, order and purchaser, blocks new allocation immediately, and leaves unrelated batches sellable; protected exports reproduce the retained facts without leaking unrelated customer data.

### FM8 — Registers, control reports and activation evidence

**Owners:** Accounting export, Reports, all producers. **Maps to:** B18, B27a-e, B32, B36/C3; CI-P4/P5. Deliver current/available/reserved/in-transit stock, item/group/warehouse/lot/cohort ledgers, near-expiry/nonmoving stock, purchase/primary-sale/secondary-sale, outlet/channel/territory, order/backorder/unbilled/unmoved, supplier scheme and expiry claims, credit ageing, tax and GL bridges. Every report uses one frozen checkpoint, dated classification, protected field scope and nonmultiplying movement/value grains. Replenishment and gross-margin reports distinguish sale price, MRP, purchase cost, landed cost and scheme benefit.

Run native-database and two-tenant rehearsals for concurrent receipt/issue/reservation/return, fiscal rollover, backdated correction, duplicate messages, unknown outcome and crash/replay. Compare rebuilt stock/value/claims/report generations to owner balances and GL; verify migrations, downgrade/restore, permissions, Audit/outbox, API contracts and generated clients. Activate by company/premises/profile only after the applicable B15-B36 gates and C3 evidence pass; B31 participates where reservation is selected. Until then capabilities and effective item reads continue to say unavailable. C4 is required only for the separately selected manufacturing/job-work profile.

### FM9 — Manufacturer, contract packer and principal-owned C&F extensions

**Maps to:** B37-B41/C4 for BOM/manufacturing/job work; PH-H and a separate agreement/tax gate for principal-owned stock. A manufacturer/packer consumes raw and packaging materials, creates finished SKU lots and by-products/scrap with genealogy, output cost, declared pack/MRP, quality release, tax and wastage evidence. Contract packing records principal versus worker ownership/custody and settlement separately from an ordinary owned-stock sale. A C&F/distributor holding principal-owned goods needs agreement, title-transfer and invoice-issuer evidence; its custody quantity is not the distributor's inventory asset. These use the same stock positions, movements and common vouchers. Neither extension blocks a verified ordinary retailer/owned-stockist release; neither is enabled by the ordinary profile's setting.

## 4. Scenario acceptance set

1. **Pack and price:** buy a case of 24, sell six each and one inner pack; revise the case ratio later; historical quantity/value remain unchanged. Two received cohorts of the same SKU and manufacturer batch with different printed MRPs are both sellable only at their respective legal ceiling.
2. **Purchase and distribution:** purchase order 100 cases, receive 60, invoice 55, reject five, transfer 20 through transit and deliver 12 against two customer orders. Order/receipt/invoice/transfer/outstanding buckets and both depots reconcile with no phantom stock.
3. **Retail sale:** scan item/pack, use cash plus digital tender and partial credit, apply a permitted invoice discount and GST; issue/refund documents reproduce accepted calculation, stock/COGS, tender and party balance after replay.
4. **Promotion and return:** issue a 10+1 deal, then return part of the paid and reward units; stock, scheme entitlement, original tax, credit note, supplier claim and gross-margin report conserve quantity and value. An unrelated gift/sample uses its own purpose/tax path.
5. **Food safety:** receive two lots with different best-before dates; FEFO picks the eligible one. Hold/recall only one lot, block competing dispatch, trace downstream recipients and prove retained distribution facts after a fiscal-year rollover.
6. **Concurrency and isolation:** two companies, two simultaneous order/reservation/return attempts, duplicate message with a new HTTP key, closed stock period and stale source projection all fail or recover deterministically without double stock, double tax or cross-company leakage.
