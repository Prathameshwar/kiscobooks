## Findings from established products

- [TallyPrime](https://help.tallysolutions.com/salesperson-details-collection-commission-tally/) uses salesperson cost-centre allocation on sales and, separately, on party/receipt entries. Its example shows why the seller of an invoice and the person collecting its debt must be different facts.
- [BUSY](https://busy.in/faqs/features/accounts/51/) enables salesperson/broker capture by configuration and voucher level. Its [help](https://busy.in/faqs/features/accounts/47/) treats salesperson and broker as one configurable master, while its [commission guidance](https://busy.in/faqs/features/accounts/17/) permits item-level rates. We should retain configurable capture but model an internal sales representative and an external broker separately; they have different identity, access, liability, and reporting rules.
- [BUSY's multi-counter retail guidance](https://busy.in/accounting-software/retail-shop/departmental-store/) calls for cashier/counter attribution, mixed tenders, and daily counter reconciliation. [Marg's counter-sale guidance](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/inventory/3273/1/How-to-set-Counter-Sale) shows a counter sale may later become a retail or wholesale bill: conversion must preserve source attribution without posting twice.
- [Odoo POS](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale/use.html) records POS register, cashier, opening/closing and cash-in/out; [Shopify POS](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/cash-register-management/register-sessions-in-shopify-pos) separately tracks register sessions, opening float, payment modes, cash movements and closing discrepancy. [Shopify permissions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/permissions/pos-permissions) distinguish sales-attribution edits from register management. These are separate operational controls, not extra ledger accounts.

## What exists and what is missing here

Identity has a `salesperson` **authorization role** (`apis/identity/src/identity/domain/system_roles.py`), but no commercial salesperson assignment master. Accounting `party_role` currently permits only CUSTOMER, SUPPLIER and OTHER. Vouchers has one shared `voucher`/`voucher_revision`, `voucher_party`, `voucher_link`, financial `voucher_tender` and bill allocations. It has no typed salesperson/broker/cashier assignment, counter, terminal, drawer or session. Its custom fields are not a safe substitute for controlled attribution. Catalog has a company-owned `sales_channel` classification and warehouses; neither is a checkout counter. Identity's branch address is an address, not an outlet operating entity. Current voucher configuration binds company/type and date; no outlet or counter scope. Number series already support prefix/suffix, financial-year reset and atomic counters, but no counter binding. Sales, goods, tax, statutory and reporting activation still depend on the open B15-B31/C2-C3 gates in `docs/voucher-delivery-tracker.md`; this plan must not imply those gates are complete.

## Domain decisions

1. **Salesperson** means a company-approved selling representative, possibly without a login. **Cashier** means the authenticated person operating a POS session. **Collector** means the person who physically or digitally collected a receipt. The same person may occupy all three roles, but the roles are stored independently. An Identity user may link to one salesperson identity; a salesperson record alone grants no access. No payroll records or salary calculation.
2. **Broker/agent** means an external commercial intermediary. Accounting owns the party and its BROKER/AGENT eligibility (an additive role migration); a broker may also be a customer/supplier. Brokerage payable is a separate accounting consequence requiring a reviewed agreement, basis, tax and approval. Merely selecting a broker does not post liability or change invoice GST.
3. **Outlet** means an operating site within one company, with a registered company address/tax-registration reference and allowed warehouses. **Counter** is a checkout lane within an outlet; **terminal** is a device; **drawer** is a controlled cash container; **session** is one open/close accountability period. None is a warehouse, sales channel, tax registration, ledger, or voucher type. One outlet may have many counters sharing stock and prices. Avoid deriving legal place of supply solely from a counter.
4. Attribution is optional by default. Enablement and requiredness are separate per company, outlet, voucher type/class and role, with dated revisions. A narrower binding can restrict, never silently expand, company policy. A disabled role is rejected in input, not quietly ignored. Historical posted documents retain their accepted policy and actor snapshots if a master is later renamed or deactivated.
5. The voucher revision remains the common document base; add one bounded typed `voucher_assignment` relation keyed by revision, role and optional line key. Avoid tables per voucher type and avoid overloading `voucher_party` with employees, brokers and counters. Counter/session/tender activity needs its own operational entities because it exists across vouchers and shifts. Keep authoritative money, tax and GL in Accounting; stock in its stock owner.

## Applicability and default behavior

| Document/profile | Seller | Broker | Collector | Counter/session |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales quote and sales order | optional/required by policy; source for conversion | optional by policy | none | optional origin counter only for in-store order |
| Sales challan/delivery | inherit selling attribution, override with reason | inherit broker, no earning by delivery alone | none | fulfilment counter only if configured; no cash session |
| Sales invoice, retail bill | capture/inherit; optional line splits | capture/inherit; possible earning source after posting | if paid at issue, capture on payment event | required only in configured POS flow |
| Sales return/credit note/refund | reference original seller for reversal; keep return handler separately | reference original broker for clawback review | refund handler separately | counter/session when refund is handled at POS |
| Receipt/advance/bill adjustment | original seller remains on source invoice; optional collection attribution | normally source-linked only; do not re-earn brokerage | capture collector, including different person | counter/session only when money goes through drawer or POS terminal |
| Payment | optional disbursement handler, never inferred seller | broker-payable settlement references approved claim | payment handler | counter/session only for controlled cash-out at a counter |
| Purchase quote/order/invoice/return | no sales seller by default | optional procurement agent profile | payment handler if later paid | no POS counter by default |
| Journal, contra, bank transfer, manufacturing/job work, stock journal | no commercial attribution by default | no brokerage by default | no collector by default | no counter unless a separately specified cash-drawer movement creates a linked money voucher |

The configuration engine should expose role mode `OFF | OPTIONAL | REQUIRED`, allowed assignment granularity `DOCUMENT | LINE`, maximum participants, inheritance rule, override permission, and applicability to each voucher kind/class. Start with one lead salesperson and one broker per document; allow multiple participants and percentage/amount allocations only when enabled. Shares on the same basis must total 100%; line and header allocations must not overlap. An unassigned order stays unassigned unless an explicit company default applies. Conversion copies a source snapshot from quote -> order -> challan -> invoice, but the new document records provenance and can be overridden before issue with a reason and authority. A later change to the order does not rewrite an issued invoice. If a quote or order splits into several invoices, each invoice gets its own attribution and earning claim, bounded by its invoiced quantity/value.

## Data and API contract

- **Identity**: `commercial_salesperson` (company, code, display name, active dates, optional user link, allowed outlet/territory/customer scopes), without HR fields. Add permissions for maintaining assignments, overrides, counter operation, cash close and variance approval. Authenticated cashier/collector IDs must be server derived where applicable; the client cannot impersonate another operator merely by supplying a UUID.
- **Accounting**: extend party-role eligibility for BROKER/AGENT; `broker_agreement` with effective dates, eligible company/outlet/customer/item or item-group, basis, rate/cap, recognition milestone, reversal and withholding/tax treatment references. Versions must not overlap on the same priority. Broker bank/tax evidence reuses party masters. Separate payable account mapping is required before any accounting posting.
- **Vouchers**: `voucher_assignment` on revision with typed role (`SELLER`, `BROKER`, `COLLECTOR`, `RETURN_HANDLER`, `PAYMENT_HANDLER`), principal ID, optional line key, share and basis, source assignment/link, evidence version, accepted name/code snapshot, and override reason. Explicitly validate same-company scope, active date, allowed voucher kind, business role and permitted master visibility. Include assignments in canonical hash, revision copy, issue/approval, audit and read/export contracts. Avoid a free-text salesperson field or custom attribute as financial authority. Optional `outlet_id`, `counter_id`, `session_id`, `terminal_id` on a shared operational context bound to the revision, with frozen issue snapshot. Public APIs: scoped master lists, config preview/effective resolution, create/edit/read assignments, attribution preview, source-inheritance preview and guarded override.
- **Counter operations**: company-owned outlet/counter/terminal/drawer masters; dated status and ownership mappings; `counter_session` with open/close actor/time, opening float, expected and counted cash, variance, approval and lock version; append-only `counter_cash_event` for float, cash-in/out, safe drop and correction with reason and linked financial voucher where one exists; tender-to-session claim keyed by payment/execution ID for exactly-once aggregation. No independent cash balance that can diverge from posted tender events; materialized session totals are rebuildable. A session is required only for configured POS tender paths. Enforce one open session per drawer, bind a terminal to one counter at a time, and require explicit cashier handover/actor history if several cashiers share a session. Opening and closing denomination capture can reuse existing cash-denomination controls.
- **Numbering**: optionally bind a sales/return/receipt series to outlet/counter and financial year. Validate global displayed-number uniqueness in the legal invoice scope even when series are counter-specific; lock the existing series counter atomically. Capturing a counter does not change legal company/GST numbering rules by itself. Manual override retains permission, reason and immutable issued number.
- **Events and reports**: publish versioned accepted-assignment and counter-session facts through the existing outbox/audit pattern. Reporting facts use explicit grains: document revenue, line quantities, collection allocation, tender, and session event. Never join many assignments to many tenders and sum invoice totals. Include effective-dated snapshots and source/reversal links so reports can be rebuilt.

## Commission and brokerage rules

Tracking and calculation must be separately switchable. Configure basis (`NET_ITEM_BEFORE_TAX`, `NET_INVOICE_BEFORE_TAX`, `COLLECTED_APPLIED_AMOUNT`) and explicit inclusion/exclusion of discounts, charges, freight, free/deal lines and tax; no implicit “invoice total” base. Define rate precedence (specific agreement -> customer/item agreement -> profile default), effective date, cap and approved override. Calculate on Accounting's accepted invoice/bill-allocation numbers and source component IDs, never by recalculating client prices. Sales-based entitlement is provisional until the configured milestone; collection-based entitlement is earned only on applied receipts, with advance application counted once. Partial collection/credit notes/returns/cancellations create linked reversals, not negative edits. A collector may receive collection credit without receiving sales credit. Where multiple salespeople/brokers share a transaction, allocate the same supported basis once, with deterministic rounding remainder.

Store `commission_claim`/`brokerage_claim` as source-linked, versioned calculation evidence with `PROPOSED -> APPROVED -> ACCRUED -> SETTLED` and reversal states. Preview never posts. Approval produces one Accounting expense/payable entry through the existing voucher execution path; settlement is a referenced Payment voucher, not a second expense. Require explicit review of Indian GST on broker service, TDS applicability and registration/invoice evidence by agreement; do not hard-code a universal tax rate or auto-self-invoice. No salesperson salary or payroll.

## Required acceptance scenarios

1. Optional company continues using existing vouchers with no extra fields or session burden; configured POS rejects missing cashier/counter/session, while back-office wholesale invoice can omit them.
2. Sales rep A quotes, B negotiates an approved reassignment, broker X introduces the customer, C collects half against two bills; invoice sales and receipt collections stay distinct.
3. Two counters at one outlet sell from a shared warehouse concurrently; invoice numbers remain unique, stock and GST post once, drawers reconcile independently; each terminal retry is idempotent.
4. A return at a different counter reverses only the corresponding original sale/brokerage share; refund tender reduces the correct session and does not rewrite the original invoice.
5. Cash float, cash sale, UPI/card, cash-out, refund and safe drop produce a reviewable expected-versus-counted drawer difference; bank settlement/reconciliation does not double count the counter collection.
6. A broker is deactivated or an agreement rate changes after issue; historical reports and approved claims remain reproducible. A late credit note creates a traceable reversal in the correct fiscal period.
7. Duplicate, stale, out-of-scope, unauthorized and cross-tenant actor/counter IDs fail closed. Bulk imports and external order adapters follow the same validation and provenance contract.
