# Manufacturing, BOM and job-work backend implementation plan

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Status:** Detailed implementation plan; no manufacturing or job-work effect is activated by this document. **Scope:** raw materials, intermediates, finished goods, co-products, by-products, recoverable scrap, process loss, in-house production and both directions of job work. UI and payroll/HR are excluded. This refines [Phase 6](voucher-phase6-execution.md), [all-kind configuration](voucher-configuration-design.md), [stock recording](voucher-stock-recording-reporting-design.md), [tax recording](voucher-tax-recording.md) and the [delivery tracker](voucher-delivery-tracker.md); B37-B41 and C4 retain their existing gates.

## 1. Product model and current baseline

TallyPrime permits multiple BOMs for an item, a Manufacturing Journal based on a Stock Journal, and explicit component, co-product, by-product and scrap entries. Job-work orders and Material In/Out have separate principal and worker directions; a principal can configure consumption either on Material In or in a separate entry. These are the entry patterns to support, not evidence that our existing APIs execute them. [TallyPrime manufacturing](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/inventory/manage-inventory-in-manufacturing-tally/), [job-work setup](https://help.tallysolutions.com/job-work-setup/), [Material In consumption](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/inventory/inventory-and-manufacturing-faq/).

The repository currently has an exact, **non-posting** Catalog BOM quantity preview and a **non-posting** Accounting multi-output cost preview. It has shared Vouchers types, policy revisions, item lines, references and source evidence. It does **not** have a published Catalog BOM/process aggregate, guarded manufacturing execution, job-work custody/WIP owner, stock genealogy or filing-ready job-work tax export. Do not infer readiness from the previews or configuration schema. B37 is in progress for preparatory definition work; B38-B41 remain planned.

**Implementation checkpoint (2026-10-11):** Vouchers now has bounded schema-v2
`manufacturing` and `job_process` policy shapes, sparse restrictive inheritance,
profile filtering and fixed job-work role validation. It refuses combined
consumption until an atomic stock owner exists. No Catalog BOM persistence,
publication, job/production capture, stock execution or tax effect follows from
this policy representation. B37 remains in progress; B38-B41 remain planned.

Selected baseline: **one-level BOM**, one primary output plus distinct co-products/by-products/scrap, multiple named BOM revisions for a finished item, sequential process steps, actual-input override with reason, in-house and principal/worker jobs, separate or combined consumption, owned WIP, genealogy and actual multi-output cost. **FIFO and moving-average valuation are both required** under D02. Reject multi-level expansion, substitution and recursive BOM use in this release; they need a separate versioned contract. A SKU's role is contextual: an intermediate may be an output of one run and input to another, and a by-product may be sold later. Do not encode one permanent global `RAW_MATERIAL`/`FINISHED_GOOD` role as an execution constraint.

## 2. Ownership and configuration layers

| Layer | Owner and durable facts | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Product/process definition | Catalog: item/unit/inventory profile, BOM identity and immutable revisions, input/output/operation rows, effective selection, publication and consumer release | A BOM is an expectation and source snapshot; publication or preview never changes stock, WIP or GL. |
| Voucher capture | Vouchers: common voucher/revision/line aggregate, typed process/run, source/lot/party/challan evidence, policy digest, job-order and Material In/Out claims | No separate voucher-header table per kind. A document is not an Accounting result. |
| Physical and financial owner | Accounting: guarded stock/custody positions, cost origins/layers or average pools, WIP, immutable quantity/value/GL legs, output cost, genealogy, source claims and reversal receipts | Only this owner commits quantity, value, bill or tax effects. Captured value, BOM rate or printed zero is never an owner valuation. |
| Reporting | Reports: stock/custody/WIP/variance/age/ITC-04 working projections from committed owner facts and immutable source snapshots | A report cannot derive ownership from warehouse name or multiply value through genealogy joins. |

Use the existing company/type/class policy revision and dated binding framework. Add **code-defined, typed** `manufacturing` and `job_process` subpolicies in schema v2 with descriptor metadata: allowed kind/profile, value type, merge rule, document/stock/posting/statutory date basis, required Catalog/Accounting capability, enforcement stage and readiness flag. `COMMON` can narrow compatible settings; unsupported kinds ignore a common subpolicy without changing their digests. Type/class can only narrow executable permissions and supply valid defaults. Preserve v1 wires/hashes and pin resolved policy and Catalog source revisions on each accepted document. No administrator-defined formula, executable custom attribute or untyped JSON recipe may drive quantity, cost, tax or posting.

### 2.1 Manufacturing settings

| Setting group | Typed choices and default | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| BOM choice | Published revision required for `STOCK_JOURNAL/MANUFACTURING`; permitted BOM IDs or item/category scope; default revision only if uniquely effective; direct manual recipe is a later gated profile | Effective date, item/unit, release acknowledgement, material/operation availability and current company checked at capture and execution. No silent fallback to a different BOM. |
| Run and quantity | Run-count requirement when fixed-per-run inputs exist; requested good quantity; base-unit conversions; actual-input variance reason; per-component loss and primary/output yield tolerance | Preview shows expected and actual separately. Four-place quantities must be exactly representable; no rounding that invents stock. Tolerance overage needs approval, not an automatic balancing leg. |
| Input selection | Allowed source locations, lots/serials, expiry/hold handling, own versus principal-owned material, source-claim mode and permitted WIP destination | Accounting verifies available residual, ownership, valuation method and immutable input slices. No negative-stock relaxation from this configuration. |
| Outputs | One primary plus bounded distinct co-products/by-products/scrap; destination/inspection/hold location; recoverable versus nonrecoverable scrap; output lot/serial/manufacture/expiry evidence | Every stocked output has item/unit/lot identity and positive quantity. Pure process loss is a variance reason, not a fabricated scrap stock item. |
| Cost | FIFO or moving-average source valuation as selected by the effective Catalog item inventory profile, explicit capitalizable processing/overhead and nonrecoverable-tax sources, output allocation basis/weights, variance and write-off accounts | Voucher policy may narrow eligible profiles but cannot switch valuation per transaction. Owner computes actual cost and GL bridge. By-product credit or cost share must be explicit, nonnegative and reconcile to the same total; no arbitrary journal can add finished units. |
| Process | Published sequential operation revision, stage completion rule, partial run and rework policy, allowed completion date and approval thresholds | Stage movement is an explicit owner operation if it changes WIP/custody; stage notes alone have no stock or cost effect. ### 2.2 Job-work settings by voucher profile

| Kind/profile | Required configuration | Accepted effect |
|---|---|---|
| `JOB_WORK_OUT_ORDER` | Principal direction, worker eligibility, process/BOM and expected inputs/outputs/scrap, due dates, permitted direct/order-linked flow, service terms and source schedules | Commitment only. No custody, payable, GST or manufacturing effect. |
| `JOB_WORK_IN_ORDER` | Worker direction, principal eligibility and material ownership, expected output and service terms, receiving/dispatch sites | Commitment only. Principal-owned material remains third-party property in our books. |
| `MATERIAL_OUT` principal | Raw/component dispatch, original source lot, worker/custodian/site, legal challan requirements and optional order | Guarded movement to own-at-worker custody; beneficial ownership and cost basis remain with principal. Printed zero commercial value does not mean zero inventory value. |
| `MATERIAL_IN` principal | Finished/co-/by-product/scrap receipt, source dispatch slices, acceptance/rejection location and `SEPARATE`/`COMBINED` consumption | Guarded return/receipt. In combined mode the same Accounting command consumes exact source input slices and creates outputs; in separate mode it requires a previously committed consumption result. |
| `MATERIAL_IN` worker | Principal's raw-material receipt, ownership/custodian/source challan, held/inspection location | Third-party custody only; no purchased inventory asset or ITC from receiving that property. |
| `MATERIAL_OUT` worker | Finished/scrap return, source input/output claim and principal identity | Reduce third-party custody once. Service billing remains a separate Sales invoice/receipt. |

Freeze principal/worker direction and beneficial owner in each issued type/profile. A company acting in both roles needs distinct type policies, even when the same Material In/Out kind is used. Direct flow without an order is permitted only under a published direct-flow policy with full source/challan evidence. `COMBINED` cannot be published until the atomic owner recipe is certified; a setting alone cannot consume stock. The consumption location and exact prior dispatch/input slices are mandatory. TallyPrime documents the direction and consumption switches; our once-only source claims are a platform control. [TallyPrime job-work setup](https://help.tallysolutions.com/job-work-setup/), [Material In consumption](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/inventory/inventory-and-manufacturing-faq/).

## 3. Proposed native data and API contracts

**Catalog definition:** `bom`, `bom_revision`, `bom_input`, `bom_output`, `bom_operation`, `bom_effectivity` (or equivalently named normalized tables) keyed by tenant/company and immutable revision. Input stores item/version, base unit/conversion, quantity per basis or per run, loss basis and allowed source. Output stores item/version, role, nominal quantity/yield, cost-allocation rule and expected stock treatment. Operation stores sequence, process identity and optional external-service reference. Publish requires one primary, distinct output SKUs, no same-SKU input/output, bounded rows, exact units, no recursion/substitution, no overlapping effective assignment for the same selection key, and acknowledged source projections. Draft/revise/publish/retire, preview requirements and published-revision read APIs are required; retirement cannot rewrite referenced runs.

**Vouchers capture:** reuse `voucher`, `voucher_revision`, `voucher_line`, item detail, party and source-link tables. Add shared typed process/run details and line-role/source-claim rows only where optional sections need them: pinned BOM/process revision/hash, run count, requested/actual quantity, `INPUT|PRIMARY|CO_PRODUCT|BY_PRODUCT|SCRAP|LOSS`, lot/serial/location/owner/custodian, input source slice, output genealogy allocation, variance reason and source document/challan references. Job order schedules and material claims use immutable revision+line+schedule IDs. The same tables serve Stock Journal manufacturing and Material In combined production; no per-kind document headers. Proposed endpoints: effective configuration and BOM preview, job-order issue/short-close, material movement preview/execute, manufacturing preview/execute/reverse and source residual/read APIs. Exact route names follow existing conventions and generated clients when implemented.

**Accounting owner:** introduce durable cost-origin and custody/WIP facts alongside the existing stock ledger design: source quantity/value legs, own-at-worker versus third-party positions, WIP input and output/value legs, owner command receipt, bounded claim key, lot genealogy edge and GL bridge ID. Store the selected FIFO layer IDs or moving-average pool generation and the exact cost taken from each source. All owner commands carry company/actor authority, policy/source hashes, expected versions, posting/physical/statutory dates as applicable, idempotency key and exact source claims. Lock claims and affected positions atomically. Replays return one result; failure writes neither partial output nor partial input consumption. Corrections append reverse and replacement facts under open-period rules.

**Reports:** project from committed owner receipts at a complete event barrier. Add finished and raw stock by item/group/location/lot, input consumption, production yield and variance, co-/by-product/scrap, WIP roll-forward, material-at-worker, third-party stock from principal, original-dispatch age, genealogy trace and job-order open/completed status. Include configuration/BOM/claim/owner generation in drill-down evidence. Rebuild and frozen-run reads must match live projections without double counting a cost that has multiple genealogy links.

## 4. Execution and accounting invariants

1. **Definition versus actual:** a BOM or order derives expected quantities only. Execution records actual source slices and output quantities. A requisition/job order does not consume stock; a Material Out/Stock Journal does so only through an accepted Accounting receipt.
2. **Quantity:** each input and output is conserved against its own SKU/base unit and source cap. Never require the numerical sum of unlike input/output quantities to balance. Require actual yield/loss/scrap classification, reason and tolerances. An output SKU cannot also be consumed by the same baseline run.
3. **Value:** `capitalizable output + closing owned WIP + recognized loss/expense = consumed owned input cost + eligible processing/overhead + nonrecoverable input tax + opening owned WIP`, with explicit adjustment/reversal legs. Recoverable scrap/by-product gets an explicit nonnegative cost share or credit within this bridge. Expense or noncapitalizable charge is not hidden in finished stock. Accounting uses the effective item-profile FIFO or moving-average method; a voucher choice or manual rate cannot override the owner origin silently.
4. **Ownership and custody:** own stock at a worker remains an asset in a separate custody/WIP position; principal-owned goods held by us remain memorandum custody, excluded from our inventory value and balance sheet. Processing a principal's material cannot turn it into our finished-goods asset. Own added materials and service charges are separate measured flows.
5. **One consumption:** each source lot/receipt slice has a single capped consumption ledger. Separate Stock Journal consumption and combined Material In consumption compete for the same claims; the second must fail. Returns, onward job worker moves, rejected output and shortage reference the original dispatch/input slices.
6. **GL/tax:** a physical movement is not automatically a sale or purchase. Capitalized cost and WIP control entries reconcile to owner stock value; processing-service Purchase/Sales invoice and Payment/Receipt use existing invoice/money owners, linked to job source and never reissue physical stock. No generic Journal may manufacture stock. GST/VAT treatment comes from an effective tax profile and statutory dates, not a BOM flag.
7. **Financial year:** retain original dispatch/lot age and cross-year WIP/custody claims; opening positions do not duplicate prior movement. Late service bills, input corrections and returns use supported current posting period with original-source evidence. Closed-period facts remain immutable.

Example for acceptance: consume owned material at actual cost 100.0000 and capitalize an eligible external processing charge 20.0000. With explicit output weights 9:1, the owner allocates 108.0000 to finished goods and 12.0000 to a recoverable by-product. If 5 units of physical waste are unrecoverable, record that quantity as process loss; do not create a scrap asset or subtract 5 from a different SKU's quantity. A separate noncapitalizable charge remains expense and cannot be smuggled into the 120.0000 pool. The exact weights and charged costs are pinned in the accepted owner receipt.

## 5. India GST and document evidence

For each principal-owned dispatch, retain the principal/worker registrations, original challan identity/date, input versus capital-good category, direct-to-worker/onward legs, source lot/quantity/value, receipts and supplies from the worker's site. Maintain job-service invoices independently. Section 143 and related rules distinguish returned/supplied goods, time-limit cases and certain tool categories; waste/scrap may have a different supplier depending on worker registration. These are **effective-rule inputs** for the tax owner, not hard-coded universal outcomes. The first release produces a reviewable overdue/deemed-supply case with remaining source slices and dates; it does not automatically issue a tax invoice or post GST. Any ITC-04 working export must declare its supported period/profile and be verified against then-current portal/rule requirements before filing-ready status. [CGST job-work circular](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/circularno-38-cgst.pdf), [CGST Act section 143](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/CGST-bill-e.html), [TallyPrime principal job work](https://help.tallysolutions.com/principal-manufacturer/).

Keep operational voucher number, statutory challan number, supplier/job-worker document number and processing-service tax invoice number distinct. Legal challan/transport requirements are D05/D09 owner gates. Do not let zero-value display or an operational number satisfy legal movement evidence by itself.
