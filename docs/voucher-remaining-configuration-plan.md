# Remaining voucher-type configuration implementation plan

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Status:** reviewed backend plan; a [scoped v2 backend slice](voucher-configuration-v2-execution.md) is implemented, while the remaining packages and UI are separately gated. **Scope:** the 21 non-`SALES` base kinds and their supported profiles, plus provisional `SALES/PRO_FORMA` where it shares the general non-posting controls. Ordinary and POS Sales invoicing stays in the separate [Sales plan](voucher-sales-configuration-plan.md). Read with the [all-kind configuration design](voucher-configuration-design.md), [delivery tracker](voucher-delivery-tracker.md), [tax design](voucher-tax-recording.md), and [financial-year contract](voucher-financial-year-design.md). This plan refines the existing 45 batches; it creates no new voucher kind or per-kind header table.

## 1. TallyPrime findings and translation

The official TallyHelp pages describe configurable derived voucher types, numbering, optional entry, narration, default accounting allocations and type-specific behavior. Payment/Receipt entries support single or double entry, bank allocation and preselected bill references. Orders link to receipt/delivery notes and allow partial closure; stock journals have transfer and manufacturing modes; job-work setup distinguishes principal/worker and Material In/Out behavior. These are product observations, not claims that our platform already executes them. See [voucher types](https://help.tallysolutions.com/voucher-types-tally/), [payments and receipts](https://help.tallysolutions.com/knowledgebase/payments-and-receipts-tally/), [sales orders and delivery notes](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/sales-process/sales-order-tally/), [purchase orders and pre-closure](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/purchase-process/purchase-order-tally/), [inventory vouchers](https://help.tallysolutions.com/inventory-entry-tally/), [job-work setup](https://help.tallysolutions.com/job-work-setup/) and [bank reconciliation setup](https://help.tallysolutions.com/bank-recon-setup/).

Tally may let an optional or post-dated voucher appear in selected reports before it becomes an actual entry. Here provisional, scheduled and actual effects must be distinct, with a fresh authorization and fiscal check when an actual effect is committed. Tally's entry-screen options are presentation defaults; they cannot bypass account, stock, tax, custody or source-cap rules. A bank-statement row is an observation, not a posted voucher. [Post-dated cheque behavior](https://help.tallysolutions.com/post-dated-cheque-managment-tally/), [bank statement import](https://help.tallysolutions.com/bank-statement/).

## 2. Platform baseline and configuration architecture

The code-defined registry currently contains **22 kinds**. Quotation is `SALES_ORDER/QUOTATION`, indent/requisition is `STOCK_JOURNAL/REQUISITION`, delivery challan is `DELIVERY_NOTE/DELIVERY_CHALLAN`, and bank payment/receipt/transfer are existing `PAYMENT`, `RECEIPT` and `CONTRA` kinds, not extra bank voucher kinds. `PAYMENT/ADVANCE` and `RECEIPT/ADVANCE` are existing profiles. Job-work orders and material movements are four distinct kinds. Company-created types may derive from an implemented kind/profile, but cannot invent executable posting semantics. [Registry](../apis/vouchers/src/vouchers/domain/registry.py).

Existing Vouchers `Policy` v1 supplies common modes, sections, limits, party roles, approval, dates, series/templates, `MoneyPolicy` and `InvoicePolicy`. Company policy families are currently `COMMON`, `SALES`, `PURCHASE`; type revisions and classes can narrow policy. `MoneyInput` already distinguishes `GENERAL`, `TRANSFER`, `SETTLEMENT`, `ADVANCE`, `REFUND`, `BILL_ADJUSTMENT`; native `MoneyPolicy` controls account kinds/IDs, tender modes, tracking, recognition, splits, allocations and journal cash/bank. Vouchers has non-posting order/quotation/requisition terms and planned quantities, but lacks complete schedule/source claims. Accounting owns ordinary money/instrument/bill effects; stock/cost, statutory invoice, jobs and many fulfillment paths remain behind their batch gates. The shared issue series has basic automatic/manual/override settings, but no immutable dated rule revision or legal issuer/category scope.

Implement a **code-defined setting registry** with typed `money`, `order`, `fulfillment`, `stock`, `job_process`, `general`, `banking` and `output` **subpolicies**, reusing common company/type/class revision and binding storage. These names are not new binding-family values: the current resolver recognizes `COMMON` and the kind code (`SALES` or `PURCHASE` where applicable), while the create schema currently permits only `COMMON`, `SALES`, `PURCHASE`. Initially place the new subpolicies in `COMMON` and narrow them by compatible type/class revisions. A separately bindable family requires a coordinated schema, resolver, publication, history and client migration with tests; simply adding enum values would make a policy silently ineffective. A setting descriptor must declare allowed kinds/profiles, value type, default, merge rule, business-date basis, required master/capability, capture/issue/post stage, and owner readiness. Keep v1 policy revisions readable and preserve their canonical hashes; schema v2 is additive and pinned per accepted document. No administrator-defined executable key, formula, SQL or arbitrary conditional expression is allowed.

Resolution: compiled profile and company capability → dated company envelope → type revision restrictions → compatible class defaults → master evidence and actor authority. Allowed sets intersect, numerical ceilings tighten, requiredness can only increase, and deny wins. Defaults must belong to the final allowed set; a class cannot widen the type or change ownership/tax effects. **Presence-aware inheritance is a prerequisite:** the current `Policy` serializer and `intersect` function cannot distinguish an omitted child default from an explicit `False`, empty string or default mode for every field. Define `UNSET` versus explicit values, and merge restrictions separately from defaults. In particular, inherited `default_mode`, `default_narration`, optional reference defaults and `MoneyPolicy` booleans must survive an omitted child field; explicit `False` must remain a restriction where applicable. Preserve historical v1 digest/replay semantics rather than silently interpreting old stored JSON through the new resolver. Reject contradictory policies at validation/publication, including required and forbidden sections, unavailable master references, impossible account groups, unsupported stock modes, circular sources, overlapping dated bindings and an enabled option without its owner implementation. Provide a preview of effective settings and origin before publication. Capture chosen values, source revisions and effective digest on the common voucher revision; a superseding policy requires pending approval to be revalidated, while issued/posted history retains its accepted snapshot.

The current `effective()` call selects bindings by `revision.document_date` for every setting. V2 must supply an explicit date context and use the descriptor's basis: document/offer date for quote and order terms, effect/posting date for money, stock-effective date for movements/counts, statutory document or supply date for tax/transport, and bank value/clearance date only for reconciliation status. Credit-terms effective date is independent of these. If an applicable date is missing, do not silently substitute today's date. Recheck company-year readiness and live security at actual execution; retain the selected dated rule revision so late prior-year work and current-year entry can coexist without changing historical outcomes.

Every family uses the common `voucher`/`voucher_revision`/`voucher_line` aggregate. Reusable typed details and source-claim/operation tables are permitted where cardinality or atomic guards demand them; **no table per voucher kind** and no generic EAV table for executable settings. Existing typed custom header/item attributes remain available through the Identity/Catalog definition mechanism, with datatype, requiredness, search and field permissions. Custom attributes cannot substitute for statutory, amount, bill, stock or custody fields.

The money form may offer single-entry and debit/credit layouts, but both normalize to the same account/tender/allocation facts. Preallocating bills changes the order of data entry, not which bills were actually selected. Tally documents those entry choices and bill-wise options in [payments and receipts](https://help.tallysolutions.com/knowledgebase/payments-and-receipts-tally/). Each party group filter must resolve Accounting group ancestry and active account version, not compare labels such as “Sundry Creditors.”

**Bank overlays:** A bank voucher is a Payment, Receipt, Contra or explicitly authorized Journal carrying bank-instrument evidence. Configure per-bank allowed modes, default voucher type/series for reviewed statement-created drafts, cheque range/leaf eligibility, PDC recognition policy, bank-reference format, candidate-match tolerance and approval. A post-dated instrument must distinguish booking, maturity, value and clearance dates; changing its status cannot repeat or silently defer an already committed Accounting effect. B43 mapped CSV import is preview → reviewed commit; statement debit/credit signs, bank account/currency and date format are mandatory. Candidate tolerance ranks suggestions only; accepted partial/grouped matches require exact amounts and never auto-post a difference or write-off. Charges or interest become proposed common vouchers. B44 cheque-book leaf transitions and cash-denomination sum rules remain Accounting/Vouchers owner controls respectively. B42 Razorpay is separately configured for verified receipt capture and a separately linked refund effect; it may create an Accounting effect only after verified capture and dedupe. See [Phase 7 contract](voucher-phase7-execution.md) and [Tally bank setup](https://help.tallysolutions.com/bank-recon-setup/).

| Accepted action | GL/bill | Stock/cost/custody | Tax and statutory evidence |

| Purchase invoice or source-linked note | Payable/credit/settlement and expense/asset bridge | Direct movement once, or claim prior receipt/return; landed-cost bridge as supported | GST/VAT/ITC/RCM and adjustment components use effective registration/date/profile and original component claims |
| Payment/Receipt, including advance | Cash/bank and exact open-item/advance effects | None | No inferred output/input tax from ordinary settlement; advance, withholding or statutory remittance uses an explicitly supported tax purpose and effective rule |

Every effect stores document, posting, stock and statutory dates separately when applicable. Reports reads committed Accounting facts at a complete owner barrier; a configured field that is visible on a form does not imply that its tax or balance producer is implemented.

## 7. API, storage, stages and verification

1. **Registry and schema:** Add typed descriptors and policy submodels with explicit compatibility/version migration. Expose applicability, defaults, restrictions, required facts and readiness via `/voucher-capabilities` and contextual effective-configuration preview. Extend company `voucher-configurations`, type revisions/classes and dated bindings rather than adding kind-specific settings endpoints. Return source revision IDs and a deterministic hash. Reject unknown/unsupported keys and incompatible controls at validate/publish.
2. **Reusable persistence:** Use existing policy profile/revision/binding and type/class rows. Add normalized child rows only for bounded reference sets, schedules, source claims, custody, bank instruments, count observations or atomic uniqueness/remaining-balance guards. Reuse common voucher details and source evidence; one migration DDL per revision, model/schema/client sync and historical v1 replay. Do not place credentials in voucher policy or mirror Accounting balances in Vouchers.
3. **Capture and action:** Draft validates structure, references and tenant scope; submit validates stage-required fields; approve validates resolved defaults, source/master versions and segregation; issue fixes document identity and immutable accepted configuration; post/actualize rechecks current actor authority, fiscal/stock/tax readiness, source residuals and owner capability. A configuration hash mismatch requires a new revision/reapproval. Non-posting issue never emits money, stock, tax or reservation events.
4. **Numbering and FY:** Shared Vouchers series may number operational orders, quotes, indents and non-statutory note copies after a compatible dated-rule revision is published. A delivery/job-work challan may also carry a Vouchers tracking number, but that is not its statutory challan identity. Where the effective GST/transport profile requires a legal challan number, D05/Accounting's legal document owner must issue and validate it with the applicable date, fiscal scope and uniqueness before release; do not reuse the operational number by assumption. Payment/Receipt/Journal accounting document IDs and Sales/Credit/Debit Note legal tax numbers stay with Accounting's D05 allocator; external supplier references are separate. Fiscal period follows the appropriate business/effect date, with late prior-year documents and current-year processing distinct. No cancellation, close or rule change recycles an issued legal number.
5. **Output/follow-up:** Pin compatible template, legal title/terms, transport fields, copy policy and rendering source facts. Artifact creation is independent of issuance, dispatch, money posting, provider execution and notification. A failed print/share cannot repeat an already committed effect.

**Minimum test matrix for every setting family:** company/type/class restriction intersection; incompatible default rejection; wrong-company/retired master; active account/group and item/lot version; allowed versus forbidden sections; permission and field denial; stage-requiredness; stale approval/config/master; backdated/current-year and year rollover; idempotent retry/concurrent command; source cap; cancellation/reversal; no effects from non-posting modes; Accounting result and Reports rebuild parity. Native MariaDB migration/model parity, downgrade/upgrade, generated client, mypy/Ruff, coverage and cross-service owner tests are required before a batch is VERIFIED. Tax and stock acceptance also require period and origin/cost reconciliation, not only API response tests.
